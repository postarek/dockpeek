Welcome to DockPeek

DockPeek shows live previews of an app's windows when you hover its icon in the Dock. Click a preview to bring that window to the front, or use the × button to close it.

  • Installs DockPeek.app in your Applications folder.
  • Adds a DockPeek pane at the bottom of System Settings.
  • Starts automatically when you log in (you can turn this off from the menu bar icon).

Requires a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
